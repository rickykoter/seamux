# 0045. On a Dock card the header jumps to the workspace and the caret folds

Date: 2026-10-31

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The user dropped the coloured rail and asked for the header to be a clearer jump target. The header folds today, so folding moves to a larger caret.

## Decision

On a Dock card the header jumps to the workspace and the caret folds

## Consequences

A click on a card's name switches workspace instead of folding it. Folding needs the caret. The tier colour stays on the badge and the dot; only the needs-you card keeps a coloured ground.
